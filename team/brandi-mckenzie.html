I am a Licensed Clinical Social Worker with more than fifteen years of experience in social work, behavioral health, and public service. My career has been dedicated to improving outcomes for adults, youth, families, and communities through clinical practice, systems leadership, and advocacy.

I am also the Founder and Lead Therapist of Elevate Counseling & Wellness, where I provide evidence-based therapy for adults and teens specializing in anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, life transitions, and perinatal mental health.

Throughout my career, I have supervised graduate social work interns, mentored emerging professionals, facilitated support groups, and worked to strengthen systems of care for vulnerable populations.
