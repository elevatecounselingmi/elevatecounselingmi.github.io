Therapist, Elevate Counseling & Wellness · Limited Licensed Master Social Worker

Maybe you’re in a season of life where you’re asking yourself, “What now?” You may be navigating changes in your career, relationships, school, or identity and feeling unsure about your next step. Maybe you’re used to having it all together for everyone else, but privately you feel overwhelmed, stuck, or unsure of what you actually want.

In therapy, I offer a supportive and collaborative space where you can slow down, talk through what’s happening, and better understand yourself and your needs. Together, we can identify your strengths, explore patterns that may no longer be serving you, and work toward realistic changes that align with the life and relationships you want.

You don’t have to have everything figured out before starting therapy. If you’re looking for a space to feel heard, gain clarity, and begin taking meaningful steps forward, I invite you to reach out to learn more about working together.
